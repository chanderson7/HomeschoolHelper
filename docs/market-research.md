# Market research snapshot

Research date: September 20, 2026. USD public advertised prices gathered during the design discussion. This is a historical snapshot, not a current-price guarantee or hands-on product evaluation. Recheck vendor pages before making commercial decisions.

## Opportunities

| Feature | Relative implementation effort after shared foundation | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Transcripts and report cards | Low–medium | Structured records and templates can provide value without high per-use compute cost |
| Attendance and instructional hours | Low | Simple entries, confirmation, aggregation and exports |
| Bulk/reusable lesson plans | Low–medium | Generate numbered lessons, paste/import lists, copy between students/years |
| Budget and expense tracking | Low | Categories, transactions, totals and export |
| Reading/field-trip/volunteer logs | Low | Dated entries feeding annual records |
| Automatic rescheduling | Medium, with significant edge cases | High workflow value if previews and recovery are trustworthy |
| Portfolios | Medium | Tagged work samples and document assembly; attachments add lifecycle cost |

Effort estimates are engineering judgments, not delivery estimates. Authentication, persistence, migration, accessibility, support and security remain necessary shared costs.

Panda's monthly Essentials-to-Pro difference is $3/month ($36 across twelve monthly payments), but the upgrade includes several features. It does not mean transcripts alone cost $36. EZHomeschool could include transcripts and budgeting in a base package without claiming feature-for-feature equivalence.

## Differentiation and pricing

Price alone is weak differentiation because free and $15–$20/year competitors already exist. Focus on rapid setup, next-lesson mode, recovery from missed days, and records produced from the same underlying activity.

At the proposed $29/year household price, annual cost would be about 41% below Manager, 52% below Syllabird Essential, and 66% below Planet's snapshot annual price. It would still exceed the lowest-cost alternatives. Validate price and operating economics rather than treating this comparison as a market forecast.

## Features to defer

Publisher plan marketplaces require content and relationships beyond software. Planet advertises thousands of plans, typically sold separately with selected annual-member benefits; see its [FAQ](https://homeschoolplanet.com/help/). Full curricula, AI tutoring, social networks, co-op administration and compliance guarantees add ongoing obligations that are poorly suited to a low-cost first release.
